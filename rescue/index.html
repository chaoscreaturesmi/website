Safe Space for Keepers

Our Zero-Judgment Promise

Life happens, situations change, and sometimes taking care of a pet becomes overwhelming. If you have an animal you can no longer care for—whether it is a tiny mouse, a parrot, a dog, a cat, or a reptile—we will take them in with zero questions asked.

There is no shame, no guilt, and no lecture here. If you cannot get to us, we will arrange a local pickup. We only care about helping you and keeping the animal safe.

  • ✔
    Zero Questions Asked No explanation required. We focus on the welfare of the animal.
  • ✔
    No Lectures, No Guilt We provide a completely safe, welcoming, and judgment-free environment.
  • ✔
    Free Local Pickup Service If transportation is an obstacle, we will arrange a safe, local pickup.
Core Operations

What We Do

From rehabilitation and strictly isolated intake areas to wildlife coordination, we support creatures of every description.

❤️

Extra-Care Rehabilitation

We specialize in animals that need a little (or a lot) of rehabilitation, medical care, patience, and love. We don't turn away behavioral cases or complex medical conditions.

🛡️

Strict Quarantine Protocols

To protect our current rescue residents and the community, all incoming animals go immediately into our dedicated, completely isolated quarantine area.

🦅

Wildlife Liaison

Found a wild animal in distress? Don't panic. Reach out to us, and we will act as the liaison to safely secure them and coordinate with licensed local wildlife rehabilitators.

⚠️ Venomous Animal Note: We are strictly non-venomous at this moment while we work on securing our advanced state certifications!
